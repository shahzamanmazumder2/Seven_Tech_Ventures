About Us

A different kind of
tech company

Founded in Dhaka in 2018, Seven Tech Ventures was built on a simple belief: technology should create tangible, lasting value — not just demos. We're a full-spectrum software engineering firm that partners with startups and enterprises to bring ambitious ideas to life.

Our distributed team of 80+ engineers, designers, and strategists operates across three continents, with deep expertise in AI, cloud infrastructure, and product design.
